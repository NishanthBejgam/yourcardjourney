Privacy Policy

mini statement for Android

Last updated 11 November 2026

mini statement has no account and no sign-in, and we do not know who you are.

The app shows short credit card news stories. It shows ads to free users, and Google (through AdMob) handles those. This page explains exactly what happens, in plain words.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or card details. There is no account, and no analytics or tracking tool of our own in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your saved stories, loved stories, light or dark choice, reading position and settings are stored on your phone only. You can save up to 10 stories, and each is removed automatically 72 hours after you save it. We never receive these as a profile. Clear the app's data or uninstall it and they are gone.

What our server sees

  • Loading stories. To show the feed, the app asks our server for the latest stories. Like any internet request, it carries your IP address and device type. Our host, Cloudflare, uses this to deliver the app and protect it from abuse. We do not use it to identify or profile you.
  • Opening your saved list. The app sends the numbers of the stories you saved so the server can return them. These numbers are not linked to you or stored against you.
  • Error logs. We may keep short-lived technical logs to fix problems. They are not used for anything else.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Free users see an occasional card marked Sponsored between stories. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third party. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device information (such as device model, operating system version and approximate location from your IP address);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps);
  • diagnostic information such as crashes and performance.

We do not give AdMob your saved stories or anything you read. Google's handling of this data is described in how Google uses data from ads and the Google Privacy Policy.

You are in control: on your phone, open Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by phone) to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. Or remove ads completely with Plus, below. Plus users are not shown ads and no ad request is made.

Plus purchases

Plus (quarterly or lifetime) is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your card or payment details. The app only learns whether your Google Play account has an active Plus purchase, so it can switch ads off.

Sharing a story

When you tap Share, the story picture is made on your phone and handed to your phone's own share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice; we never receive it.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission to load stories and ads, and the network state to know when you are offline. It also uses Google Play Billing for Plus. It does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app and everything stored on your phone is deleted. We keep no account or profile about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete. For ad data held by Google, use your Google account's My Ad Center controls.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
